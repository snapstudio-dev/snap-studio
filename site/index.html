Snap Studio

Prepare your Snapmaker prints, simply

The desktop workshop for your Snapmaker U1: from the file to the print, in one free app. In nine languages.

What it does

🖨

Made for the Snapmaker U1

The U1 plate, profiles and four heads are built in. Printers are found on your network and the U1 is shown in 3D: click a spool, a head, the bed or the light for its quick controls. Status and sending also work with any Klipper (Moonraker) printer.

✂️

Orca-style slicing

The Snapmaker Orca engine is downloaded once and kept up to date: no need to install Orca. Quality, infill, walls, surfaces, supports, brim and seam, with styles chosen from pictures, which you can save as your own profiles. Basic or Advanced settings. The prime tower shows on the plate in Prepare, where you place it, or one per material when materials do not bond. Then a G-code preview, layer by layer, with the time per type and the filament, before sending.

🎨

Multi-color, head by head

The spools loaded on the printer are read. Each color of a model, painted ones included, goes to the head you pick, and the 3D view shows the real spool colors. Virtual heads mix two spools into a new color, as Full Spectrum does.

📡

Live printer monitoring

Progress, layers, temperatures of the four heads and the plate, pause and cancel, and a print queue. One live camera on every screen (pinned if you like), and the Prints page keeps each print's record: timelapse, result, real time. Care & Tuning gathers the printer's controls without G-code, its maintenance and bed leveling.

✦

AI assistant

Describe a change or a new part, or start from your own STL or 3MF: Claude models it through Claude Code with your own account, in Claude Code's own sandbox, with a live 3D preview and every version kept. The results are multicolor 3MF files ready to slice.

📁

Your files and model sites

Thumbnails of your 3MF files (Orca, Bambu, Prusa, MakerWorld) and layer-by-layer G-code previews. Explore opens Snapmaker Space, the Store, the Wiki, the Forum and eight model libraries (MakerWorld, Printables, Thingiverse, Cults3D, MyMiniFactory, Thangs, Yeggi, GrabCAD) in tabs; a downloaded model goes straight to Prepare.

System requirements

  • macOS on Intel or Apple Silicon (one universal app).
  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64), with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime. The installer sets it up if it's missing.
  • Linux x86-64 (AppImage or .deb). ARM64 builds exist too, but without the slicing engine yet.
  • An internet connection the first time, to download the slicing engine (about 30 MB).
  • A Snapmaker U1 on your local network. Any Klipper (Moonraker) printer works for status and sending.

The packages aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. On first launch on macOS, right-click the app and choose Open; on Windows, click "More info" → "Run anyway".
