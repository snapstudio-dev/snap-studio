      New virtual head
      
    
      
      
      
      
        
        
      
      
      
      
        
      
        
        
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
      These two colors are very different: with opaque filaments, the print will look darker and more striped than this preview. Closer colors or translucent filaments mix better.
      
    
      Indicative preview: the mix is made layer by layer and depends on the filaments, the layer height and the light.
      
      
    
      The mix is smoothed by default (layers split between the two heads). Change it in the slice settings, Multi-material.
      
    
      
      
      
    
      
  
      

      

      
  
      
    
      A virtual head depends on its two spools
      
    
      
    
    
      
      
      
    
      
  
      

      

      
  
      
    
      Two ways to assign heads
      
    
      By object: each object chooses a head for each of its colours, under its row.
      
    
      By colour: the plate's colours, as the 3MF names them, are assigned once for every object, above the list, as Snapmaker Orca does.
      
    
      Switching keeps what agrees: a colour every object sends to the same head keeps it; one the objects disagree on waits for your choice, and the slice waits with it.
      
    
      The mode Prepare starts with is set in Settings › Prepare.
